How the reliability metrics are calculated +

For each task, let c of n recorded trials succeed. The estimator passk averages C(c,k) / C(n,k) across tasks: all k sampled trials succeed. The distinct pass@k estimator averages 1 − C(n−c,k) / C(n,k): at least one succeeds. The reporter uses exact rational arithmetic and rejects missing trials, duplicate identities, nonbinary rewards, and censored inputs.

The GPT-4o files contain four trials per task and support k ≤ 4; the sonnet-35-new files contain eight and support k ≤ 8. Neither supports the paper’s reliability curves through k = 32. Equal-domain averages give half the weight to retail and half to airline.
