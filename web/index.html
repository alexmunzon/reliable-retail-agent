02How to read this demonstration+

A transparent, local proof of concept

Every conversation, customer, order, and reservation is a scripted synthetic fixture. Both lanes invoke real local Python functions on independent in-memory fixture state. The dispatch counters and traces come from those local calls. No model, paid API, or real retailer is involved.

The baseline illustrates what an ungated scripted actor can do. It is not a measured failure of ChatReActAgent. The gated lane checks a mutating-tool allowlist, fresh prompt-bound consent, and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the tool name and complete arguments. An operation ledger prevents repeat attempts; uncertain outcomes stop without retrying.

Research context, with limits

This proof of concept targets the original τ-bench ChatReActAgent architecture. The original benchmark tasks are outdated. This demo does not run the benchmark, report a score, or claim to reproduce its results. The airline example demonstrates reuse of the gate across domains, not an airline evaluation.
